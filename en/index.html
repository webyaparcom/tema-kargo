FAQ

Questions about shipping

Short answers on delivery, damage and pricing.

On which days are deliveries made?

Weekdays and Saturdays; the time window depends on the area and load size (fictional demo information).

What should I do if a parcel arrives damaged?

Take a photo on delivery and report it with the tracking number; a claim is opened (demo text).

Are the images and data on the site real?

No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.

How are prices determined?

Priced by quote based on weight, volume and distance; there is no general price list in this demo.
